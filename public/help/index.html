Help and Guidance

How QCH Connect works when you send and receive referrals.

Send referrals through QCH Connect

Send referrals through QCH Connect. Call or email the Program contact only if you cannot find what you need here.

Find a Program or Direct Referral

On New Referral you choose how to pick the receiving Program. Both send the same kind of referral.

  • Help me find a Program (Find a Program): answer a few questions and see Programs that may fit. These are suggestions to help you choose. They are not an eligibility decision. You choose the Program, and the receiving Program decides.
  • I know the Program (Direct Referral): choose the Program from the list. Use Direct Referral if you already know the Program.

Review what QCH Connect understood

In Find a Program you can describe what the participant needs in your own words. QCH Connect proposes answers from your description and shows them to you first.

Review the proposed answers before matching. You can keep them, correct them, remove them, or add what is missing. Nothing is matched until you choose Find Programs.

Participant age

Age is used only to find Programs and is not saved with the referral.

Language and availability

Language and availability never remove a Program from consideration. A Program's languages of service are shown with its result to help you choose.

When no Program matches

No plausible match identified does not mean QCH has no appropriate Program. QCH Connect does not offer a fallback or a "closest match".

You can change your answers and search again, or use Direct Referral if you know a Program.

When something goes wrong

A technical problem is not a result. When matching or sending a referral fails on New Referral, the page says so and keeps the information you entered, so you can try again or use Direct Referral.

If a referral was not received, the page tells you that your information has been preserved and that you may safely resend. A resend does not create a second referral.

Where a referral stands

Each referral has three separate steps.

Response
Awaiting response, until the receiving Program responds. Then Responded.
Decision
Accepted, or Not appropriate. A Not appropriate decision comes with a reason, and the person who sent the referral can see it.
Service outcome
Not recorded, Resulted in service, or Did not result in service. A Not appropriate referral cannot have a service outcome.

Accepted does not mean the person received service. The receiving Program records the service outcome separately.

Corrections

The receiving Program can correct a decision or a service outcome. The earlier value stays in the audit history.

Participant information

Do not include unnecessary personal information. Enter only what the receiving Program needs to evaluate the referral.
