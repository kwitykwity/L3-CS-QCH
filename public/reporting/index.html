Rates

Acceptance rate = Accepted ÷ (Accepted + Not appropriate). Referrals still awaiting a response are left out. Resulted-in-service rate = Resulted in service ÷ (Resulted + Did not result), and accepted referrals with no outcome yet are left out. The server calculates every rate.

Timing

Time to Recorded Service uses only referrals that reached Resulted in Service. It measures from referral creation to the first recorded outcome. It is not the participant's service start date.
